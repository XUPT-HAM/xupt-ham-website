Xi'an University of Posts and Telecommunications is a multidisciplinary university with a
focus on information technology and engineering, located in the historic city of Xi'an.

## Our story

Founded in 1950 as the Shaanxi Province Posts and Telecommunications School, the institution
was renamed "Xi'an University of Posts and Telecommunications" in 2012 with approval from the
Ministry of Education. Today it spans the Chang'an and Yanta campuses, with a history of over
seventy years.

## Strengths

The university is known for communications engineering, electronics, computer networking, and
postal logistics, and serves as a key base for information and communications talent development
in Northwest China.

## A natural home for radio

Building on the school's deep roots in information and communications, radio activities have
always found fertile ground here — from signals and systems in the classroom to real on-air
contacts in the club, theory and practice meet naturally.
