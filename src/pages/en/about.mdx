XUPT HAM Society is a student-run amateur radio club at Xi'an University of Posts and
Telecommunications, organized by students who love radio, communications, and electronics. Our
mission is to promote amateur radio, spread communications knowledge, and build hands-on
skills — offering everything from station operation to antenna building, emergency
communications, and radio direction finding.

## Who we are

Our members come from communications, electronics, computer science, and more. We range from
complete beginners to seasoned "OMs" holding amateur radio operator licenses (Class A / B). We
host regular on-air sessions, tech talks, and field deployments to meet fellow enthusiasts
around the world.

## What we do

## How to join

We welcome all enrolled undergraduates and postgraduates, no experience required. Watch our
recruitment posts, or find us via the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.
