## About us

XUPT HAM Society is a student-run amateur radio club at Xi'an University of Posts and
Telecommunications. Our mission is to promote amateur radio, spread communications knowledge, and
build hands-on skills — offering everything from station operation and antenna building to emergency
communications and radio direction finding.
