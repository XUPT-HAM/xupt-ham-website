## Frequency and wavelength

Radio waves travel at roughly the speed of light, $c \approx 3 \times 10^8\,\text{m/s}$. Frequency
$f$ and wavelength $\lambda$ are related by:

$$ \lambda = \frac{c}{f} $$

For 14 MHz (the 20 m shortwave band), the wavelength is about:

$$ \lambda = \frac{3 \times 10^8}{14 \times 10^6} \approx 21.4\,\text{m} $$

So the "20 m band" is only approximate — it is actually a little longer than 20 m.

## Decibels (dB)

Power gain is usually expressed in decibels, turning multiplication into addition for easy link budgets:

$$ G_{\text{dB}} = 10 \log_{10}\!\left(\frac{P_{\text{out}}}{P_{\text{in}}}\right) $$

If the output power is 100× the input, the gain is $10\log_{10}(100) = 20\,\text{dB}$.
